High-Probability Intraday Trading System

A practical, rule-based document to consistently identify stocks every morning, trade with discipline, and exit profitably using probability and risk control — not prediction.

⸻

1. Objective of This System

This document explains how professional intraday traders consistently extract profits by:
	•	Selecting the right stocks every morning
	•	Trading only when probability is in their favor
	•	Exiting fast (target / stop-loss / EOD)
	•	Avoiding emotional and impulsive trades

Goal: Consistency, not perfection. A 60–70% win rate with strong risk control.

⸻

2. Core Philosophy (Read This First)

Successful traders do not try to be right every time.

They:
	•	Trade high-probability setups only
	•	Accept losses quickly
	•	Repeat the same process daily

“You don’t need to win every trade. You need to win more than you lose, with controlled risk.”

⸻

3. Daily Trading Workflow (Top-Down)

Step 1: Market Bias (Before Market Opens)

Check the overall market direction:
	•	Index trend (NIFTY / BANKNIFTY)
	•	Global cues (SGX, US markets)

Rules:
	•	Market bullish → Prefer BUY setups
	•	Market bearish → Prefer SELL setups
	•	Sideways → Trade fewer setups

⸻

Step 2: Morning Stock Selection (Pre-Market Scan)

Use a screener to filter tradable stocks.

Mandatory Criteria
	•	High volume (≥ 2× 10-day average)
	•	Good liquidity (easy entry/exit)
	•	ATR above average (must move)

Common Scan Conditions
	•	Gap up / gap down stocks
	•	Stocks near previous day High / Low
	•	Stocks near strong support / resistance
	•	Sector-wise strength

Result: 5–15 stocks maximum

⸻

4. Confluence – The Key to High Probability

A trade is taken only when multiple factors align.

Example BUY Confluence Checklist
	•	Market trend: Bullish
	•	Stock above VWAP
	•	Breakout above resistance
	•	High relative volume
	•	Bullish candle structure
	•	Risk-Reward ≥ 1:2

Minimum 4–6 confirmations required before entry.

⸻

5. Entry Rules (Non-Negotiable)
	•	No entry without a predefined stop-loss
	•	No chasing price
	•	Entry only at levels

Typical Entry Types:
	•	Breakout entry
	•	Pullback to VWAP
	•	Retest of support/resistance

⸻

6. Stop-Loss & Risk Management (MOST IMPORTANT)

Capital Risk Rule
	•	Risk 0.5%–1% of capital per trade only

Stop-Loss Placement
	•	Below support (for BUY)
	•	Above resistance (for SELL)
	•	Fixed % (if structure unclear)

A trader survives because of stop-losses, not targets.

⸻

7. Target Strategy

Use small, consistent targets.
	•	Target: 1× to 2× risk
	•	Partial booking allowed
	•	Trailing stop optional

Example
	•	Stop-loss: ₹10
	•	Target: ₹15–₹20

⸻

8. Time-Based Exit (EOD Rule)
	•	No overnight positions
	•	Exit all trades before market close
	•	If price stagnates → Exit at breakeven

Time is a risk in intraday trading.

⸻

9. Position Sizing Formula

Position Size = Risk Amount ÷ Stop-Loss

Example:
	•	Capital: ₹5,00,000
	•	Risk per trade: 1% = ₹5,000
	•	Stop-loss: ₹10
	•	Quantity = 500 shares

⸻

10. Trade Management Rules
	•	Max trades per day: 2–4
	•	No revenge trading
	•	Stop trading after daily loss limit hit
	•	Skip trades on emotional days

⸻

11. Why This System Works
	•	Trades probability, not predictions
	•	Losses are small and controlled
	•	Winners pay for losers
	•	Repeatable every day

This is how it appears like traders are right most of the time.

⸻

Bots follow rules. Humans break them.

13. Common Mistakes to Avoid
	•	Overtrading
	•	Changing strategy frequently
	•	Ignoring stop-loss
	•	Trading without market bias

⸻

14. Final Truth

There is no holy grail strategy.

There is only:
	•	Discipline
	•	Risk management
	•	Consistency

Professionals trade the same setup for years.
